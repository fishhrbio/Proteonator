# Frames, figures and sharing

Registered trajectory JSON binds actual frames to the exact topology hash and ordered atom identities. Supports 2–200 frames, at most 5,000 atoms, a32 MiB input and declared Å/nm units. Actual trajectories require supplied increasing ps times; ensembles have no inferred clock. Frames display in the comparison view while primary source coordinates remain unchanged.

Unaligned heavy-atom RMSD to the first frame and RMSF about the arithmetic mean include rigid movement. Imported samples do not establish equilibrium or convergence. Preserve the original protocol and evidence.

Figures controls set PNG dimensions, dark/light/transparent background, caption text/position and presets. PNG molecules are raster. Processing/RNA/profile SVG is vector. Reports and full CSV/JSON keep scientific context and methods.

Review ZIP previews its included report, figure, metadata and complete project, then writes a SHA-256 manifest. Inspect embedded sources/notes before sharing. Saved-view storyboards export numbered stills and captions tied to their source/scenario identities. WebM is a bounded camera orbit around fixed coordinates, clearly labeled as presentation rather than folding or dynamics.

A low RMSD, image or animation does not replace appropriate preparation, parameter validation, sampling, convergence or experimental support.
