Scope & coordinate policies

PDB and mmCIF coordinates are analyzed locally. Choose a model before comparing protein chains. Waters and non-protein residues are retained but hidden here and excluded from partner geometry. Display bonds are distance-inferred visual aids, not validated connectivity.

Annotations are saved in portable projects; no PTM position or effect is predicted. Compare supplied structures using a reviewed Cα mapping. Projects retain both coordinate sources and recalculate saved results when reopened.
