Selection actions
Scientific methods
Source coordinates remain immutable. PTM construction uses wwPDB chemical-component ideal coordinates anchored to the selected side chain. Supported PTM additions retain source heavy atoms. Canonical substitutions replace side chains in a retained backbone frame; terminal amidation replaces OXT with nitrogen. Constructed coordinates require independent validation.
Scenario summaries use an inclusive 4 Å heavy-atom cutoff. The contact workbench exposes its selected 2–8 Å cutoff; steric overlap screening remains a separate fixed 4 Å calculation. Distances and displacements are in Å; angles and torsions are in degrees. Geometric contacts are not binding affinity.
Local relaxation uses the explicitly selected OpenMM force field, pH, restraints and stopping criteria. Unsupported chemistry stops the calculation. A local minimum is not a prediction of equilibrium folding. A transition or overlay is a visualization of supplied states.
References: wwPDB Chemical Component Dictionary; Mol* (Sehnal et al., 2021); Gemmi; OpenMM. Context: UniProt and PDBe SIFTS. Full licenses and attributions are included with Proteonator.
